FAQs

Find answers to common questions about the Login Page Customizer app for Odoo.

1. What is the Login Page Customizer app?

The Login Page Customizer app is a comprehensive Odoo extension designed to expand the capabilities and appearance of your Odoo login interface. It lets you customize backgrounds, colors, alignment and password strength criteria — all from the Login Form settings.

2. How do I add a custom background image to my login page?

You can upload a background image directly through the "Login Form" settings in Odoo. The setting is straightforward and takes effect immediately without any additional configuration.

3. Can I change the alignment of the login form?

Yes! The Alignment Options feature lets you position the login form to the center, left, or right via the "Login Form" settings in Odoo — no coding required.

4. What does the Password Strength Criteria feature do?

The Password Strength Criteria feature helps guarantee strong authentication for user accounts. You can establish specific minimum character counts and complexity requirements (such as uppercase, numbers, special characters) to enhance security.

5. Is the module customizable and do I get updates?

Yes, the module is fully customizable to your needs. We also provide regular updates to ensure compatibility with the latest Odoo versions and to add new features based on user feedback.

Feel free to send us an email at odoo-support@zehntech.com with your questions, and we will get back to you as soon as possible.
