PREFERENCE POINT ALLOCATION:

80/20 SYSTEM (R30,000 - R500,000):
  Total Points: 100
    • Price: 80 points (criteria_type: price, weight: 80%)
    • B-BBEE: 20 points
      - Level 1-4: 20 points
      - Level 5-8: 10 points

90/10 SYSTEM (above R500,000):
  Total Points: 100
    • Price: 90 points (criteria_type: price, weight: 90%)
    • B-BBEE: 10 points
      - Level 1-4: 10 points
      - Level 5-8: 5 points

QUICK FACTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: What's the difference between 80/20 and 90/10?
A: Price weighting (80% vs 90%), preference weighting (20% vs 10%).
   Lower value procurements use 80/20 (more weight to preferences).
   Higher value procurements use 90/10 (more weight to price).

Q: Can I use Method A for an R35,000 procurement?
A: No. Method A is only for procurements < R30,000. Use Method B for
   R30,000 - R300,000.

Q: Do I always need to do the compliance check?
A: No. Only for Methods C (Competitive Bid < R50M) and D (>R50M).
   Others only if required by policy.

Q: What if I only know one supplier?
A: Use Method E (Sole Source). Requires Accounting Officer approval
   and price reasonableness verification. Deviation from PPPFA.

Q: Can briefing be skipped for Method C or D?
A: For Method C: Yes (optional). For Method D: No (mandatory).

Q: What's the minimum value for B-BBEE preference points?
A: Technically applies to all methods, but practically applied in
   Methods B-G (where B-BBEE scoring configured).

Q: Are panel suppliers competitive?
A: The panel establishment must be competitive. Subsequent call-offs
   are simplified procurement from an already-approved panel.

Q: How long does evaluation take?
A: Depends on method. Methods A-B: 1-2 weeks. Methods C-D: 4-8 weeks.
   Method E: 1-2 weeks. Method F: Days. Method G: Days (from panel).
