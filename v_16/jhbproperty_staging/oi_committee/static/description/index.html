Committee Management

"Have Your Board, Committees and Meetings Under Control"

This module is a specially designed application for executives and committees to manage and track their meetings and their interactions in any business-related operations.

Description

The Committee Management Module manages the critical administrative processes such as setting up meetings for envelops opening, technical evaluations, advisory committees and board of counselors and any operations related to business. This module allows to establish committees, hold them with qualified members, create and schedule meetings and issue reports, minutes and decisions made. This module, with the help of steering committees assists you to lead your main business-related operations, including Sales and Purchase into the right direction.

Key Features

  • High discretion to create committees.

  • Fully integrated with Purchase, Accounting, Employee and Sales etc.

  • Highly configurable committee types.

  • Ability to include steering committees in the approval workflow.

  • Ability to register, submit and track the documents of committee meetings.

  • Ability to create, schedule and hold committee meetings.

  • Ability to link steering committees to any business-related processes.

  • High flexibility in committee actions and functions.

  • Easy access to any related documents (meeting reports, technical evaluations, decisions made, attendances etc.).

  • Managing complicated workflow approvals, based on organization policies.

  • Multilevel configurable approval and rejection notification.
