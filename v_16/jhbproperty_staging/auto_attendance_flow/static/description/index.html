Odoo 16 Advanced Attendance & Overtime Management

Smart automation of check-in/out, leave tracking, overtime validation, and reporting

Key Features

  • ✅ Auto Check-in on Login: Automatically logs attendance when users log in to Odoo.
  • ⏰ Auto Check-out at 16:00: Scheduled job updates check-out time daily at 4 PM.
  • 📊 Monthly Working Hours Report: Consolidates hours and sends to line managers.
  • 🗂️ Attendance Leave Types: Dropdown to specify Sick, Study, or Family Responsibility Leave.
  • 📄 Overtime Pre-Approval: Overtime requires a pre-uploaded approval letter.
  • 📆 Weekend Overtime Rates: Separate rules for Saturday and Sunday pay rates.
  • 🚨 Overtime Limit Alerts: Notify users when thresholds are exceeded.
  • 📤 Monthly Summary: Auto-email attendance summary to line managers.
